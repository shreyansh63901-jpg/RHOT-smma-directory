About Us

Built to make agency-hunting honest again

SMMA India is a city-wise directory of social media marketing agencies (SMMAs) built for Indian business owners, D2C brands, clinics, restaurants, and coaching institutes who need a marketing partner they can trust — without spending weeks vetting agencies one by one.

The problem we're solving

Choosing a social media agency in India is harder than it should be. Portfolios are cherry-picked, follower counts are inflated, pricing is rarely disclosed upfront, and there's no easy way to compare two agencies side by side. Business owners end up relying on word-of-mouth or a rushed Google search — and often pay for it later.

How SMMA India is different

  • Transparent ranking: every listed agency is scored on a consistent, published methodology — rating, review volume, verification status, profile completeness, experience, and responsiveness — so the "Top 10" in your city means something.
  • Clear labelling: Featured or sponsored placements are always marked as such and never blended silently into organic rankings.
  • Real comparisons: filter by price, rating, city, and services, then compare shortlisted agencies side by side before you make a call.
  • Direct access: every agency profile links straight to their Instagram, website, and WhatsApp — no middlemen, no inflated introduction fees.

Where we operate

We're currently live across eight major Indian cities — Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Ahmedabad — with more cities being added as our agency network grows.
